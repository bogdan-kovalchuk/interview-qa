## Short answer

Integrated circuits are classified by equivalent gate count per chip: SSI contains up to 10–12 gates, MSI contains 12 to 100, LSI contains 100 to 10,000, and VLSI contains over 10,000 gates. Standard 74xx logic ICs belong primarily to SSI (basic gates) and MSI (decoders, counters, multiplexers). Modern microcontrollers, microprocessors, and FPGAs contain billions of transistors and fall into the VLSI class.[^floyd-digital-fundamentals]
