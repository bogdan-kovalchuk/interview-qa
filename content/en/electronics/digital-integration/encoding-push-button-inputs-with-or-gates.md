## Short answer

Each push button corresponds to one mathematical operation, producing a logic `1` when pressed. Each output bit `S0`, `S1`, and `S2` is generated by an `OR` gate connected to the buttons whose binary index has a `1` in that bit position (for example, buttons 1, 3, 5, and 7 drive `S0`). Unpressed inputs are tied to ground through pull-down resistors to prevent floating potentials.[^floyd-digital-fundamentals]
