## Short answer

The Sn63/Pb37 alloy is eutectic and melts and solidifies at 183 °C without a pasty state interval. This sharp transition makes it easier to judge when the joint has frozen, although by itself it does not guarantee a shiny or reliable joint.[^aim-sn63pb37]

## Detailed explanation

The 63/37 SnPb solder contains 63% tin (Sn) and 37% lead (Pb). This specific proportion is eutectic for the binary alloy: under equilibrium conditions, the material transitions between solid and liquid states at a single temperature, around 183 °C, rather than softening gradually across a temperature range.[^aim-sn63pb37]

For a beginner, the key advantage is how the alloy behaves during cooling. When the temperature drops below the melting point, the solder solidifies rapidly rather than lingering in a pasty state. This minimizes the critical window during which unintended component movement can disturb an unfrozen joint. Such a sharp transition makes hand soldering more predictable, though joint quality still depends on surface cleanliness, flux, adequate heating of both parts, and keeping the joint undisturbed until frozen.[^aim-sn63pb37]

A shiny surface finish is not inherently proof of joint quality resulting from the 63/37 composition. Joint appearance depends on the alloy, temperature, flux, surface finishes, and cooling rate; many properly soldered lead-free joints are naturally dull or satin. Likewise, eutectic behavior does not compensate for poor wetting, insufficient heating, or contamination. A clear distinction must be made between material properties and process outcomes.[^aim-sn63pb37]

Example: the nominal liquid-to-solid transition for Sn63/Pb37 occurs at approximately 183 °C. This is the melting point of the alloy itself, not the recommended temperature setting on the soldering iron: the tip is operated hotter to transfer heat into the joints and offset thermal losses.[^aim-sn63pb37]

**Common mistakes:**
- Labeling 63/37 merely as "low-melting" without explaining the absence of a pasty range.
- Assuming a shiny appearance guarantees a sound joint or demanding a mirror finish from every solder alloy.
- Overlooking lead safety: follow product guidelines and local regulations regarding handling, ventilation, and waste disposal.
