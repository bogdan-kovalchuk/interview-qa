## Short answer

Hot Air Solder Leveling (HASL) is economical and robust, but leaves uneven, domed solder mounds on component pads. Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold (ENIG) provides an exceptionally flat, coplanar surface essential for fine-pitch QFP and QFN packages. For high-density, high-reliability SMT designs, ENIG is the preferred industry finish.[^udemy-electronics-course]
