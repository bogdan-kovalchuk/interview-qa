## Short answer

Standard DIP integrated circuits and pin headers feature a lead pitch of 0.1 inch (100 mil, or 2.54 mm). Snapping to this grid aligns component pads directly with the primary routing axes of the board. This prevents awkward trace angles, keeps parallel tracks orderly, and simplifies routing buses.[^udemy-electronics-course][^ipc-2221b]
