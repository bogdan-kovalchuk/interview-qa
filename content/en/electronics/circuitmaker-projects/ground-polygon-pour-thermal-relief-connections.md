## Short answer

A solid connection to a large copper plane rapidly conducts heat away from the soldering iron during pin assembly. This leads to defective cold solder joints or component overheating caused by prolonged heating. A thermal relief pattern with spokes restricts heat sinking during soldering while maintaining a low-impedance electrical connection to ground.[^udemy-electronics-course][^ipc-2221b]
