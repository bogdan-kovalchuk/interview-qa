## Short answer

**Pulse Width Modulation** – a power-control method that varies the duty cycle of a rectangular signal.[^dou-embedded-interview]

Duty cycle = T_ON / T × 100%, so at 50% you get half the power; the frequency is usually fixed (e.g. 20 kHz for motors, 50 Hz for servos).

Applications: LED brightness adjustment, DC motor control (via an H-bridge), servos, DC/DC converters; generated by the MCU's hardware timer – no CPU load; example: `TIM3->CCR1 = 500;` with ARR=1000 gives 50% duty.
