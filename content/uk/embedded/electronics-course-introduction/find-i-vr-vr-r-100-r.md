## Short answer

<span class="formula">\(R_{total}\)</span> = 430 Ом. I = 3/430 ≈ 6.97 мА. VR₁ = 6.97мА × 100 = 0.70 В. VR₂ = 6.97мА × 330 = 2.30 В. Перевірка: 0.70 + 2.30 = 3.00 В ✓[^udemy-electronics-course]
